ESP32-S3-Relay-6CH Control Panel

🧷 ESP32-S3-Relay-6CH — Pin Breakout

Pins highlighted in yellow are INPUTS you can manually trigger by jumping them to GND. Relays are outputs the firmware drives.

👆 Click any yellow pin above to jump it to GND (simulate the wire).
All four input pins use INPUT_PULLUP, so they read HIGH (idle) until you connect them to GND. Click again to release. The relay cards above react in real time using the firmware's actual logic.
  • GPIO4 → GND = Water LOW switch (reservoir ran low → stops pump, turns water ON to refill, after 800ms debounce)
  • GPIO5 → GND = Water HIGH switch (tank full / level rose → shuts water off, releases the latch)
  • GPIO40 → GND = Manual OFF override (highest priority)
  • GPIO39 → GND = Manual ON override
⚠ Use a jumper wire or momentary switch between the pin and any GND pad. Never jump an input to 3.3V/5V.
